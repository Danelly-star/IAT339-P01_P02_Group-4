About Us

LunarTech is a gaming accessories company for people who spend a lot of time gaming or using their computers and want to improve both the function and appearance of their setup. At LunarTech, we believe your gaming setup should feel as good as it looks. Explore accessories with a calming, luminous aesthetic that makes every space your own.

The company offers keyboards, mice, headsets, desk mats, controllers, keyboard covers, wrist cushions, controller stands, and other setup accessories. LunarTech brings these products together for a specific audience of computer and gaming users, making it easier to build a coordinated setup in one place.
