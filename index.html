P01 Branding Definitions

What does company do or offer?

The companies potential customers are people looking to purchase accessories for their computer and gaming setups. This includes keyboards, keyboard covers, desk mats, wrist cushions, controller stands, and more. This company may entice people into purchasing its products because it contains items that serve a similar and specific consumer base (that is, people who play video games or use their computers often).

Who is the company to potential customers?

As a person, the company would be the friendly gaming ethusiast, who would know a lot about gaming setups, but would never make someone amateur feel inexperienced for asking questions. They would be welcoming, passionate and knowledgable about gaming technology while also caring about personal expression and style of the customer's dream gaming setup. Futhermore, their goal would be to help both casual and experienced gamers find accessories that compliments their needs, preferences and individual style/aesthetic without making the shopping experience feel unnecessarily complex.

Why might this company entice someone to purchase a product or service?

Customers might be enticed to purchase a product from our gaming accessories company because, it combines gaming performance with personalization to enhance their set up, while still providing the functionality they need for the best gaming experience. The website, will consist of clear product information, coordinated accessories of different visual styles, and beginner friendly information about the products to make customers purchase with a more confident understanding, without having to risk their money feeling uncertain.
