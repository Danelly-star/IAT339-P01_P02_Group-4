[What does the company do offer?] The companies potential customers are people looking to purchase accessories for their computer and gaming setups. This includes keyboards, keyboard covers, desk mats, wrist cushions, controller stands, and more. This company may entice people into purchasing its products because it contains items that serve a similar and specific consumer base (that is, people who play video games or use their computers often).
