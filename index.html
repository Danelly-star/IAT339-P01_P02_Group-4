P01 Branding Definitions

What does company do or offer?

LunarTech is a gaming accessories company for people who spend a lot of time gaming or using their computers and want to improve both the function and appearance of their setup. The company offers keyboards, mice, headsets, desk mats, controllers, keyboard covers, wrist cushions, controller stands, and other setup accessories. LunarTech brings these products together for a specific audience of computer and gaming users, making it easier to build a coordinated setup in one place.

Who is the company to potential customers?

If LunarTech were a person, it would be the friendly gaming enthusiast who knows a lot about gaming setups but would never make a beginner feel inexperienced for asking questions. They would be welcoming, passionate, and knowledgeable about gaming technology while also caring about personal expression and the visual style of a customer's dream setup. Their goal would be to help both casual and experienced gamers find accessories that complement their needs, preferences, and individual aesthetic without making the shopping experience feel unnecessarily complicated.

Why might this company entice someone to purchase a product or service?

LunarTech may entice customers because it combines gaming performance with personalization, helping customers enhance their setup while still getting the functionality they need for an enjoyable gaming or computer experience. The website will provide clear product information, coordinated accessories in different visual styles, and beginner-friendly explanations so customers can understand what they are purchasing and make decisions with greater confidence. By balancing useful gaming gear with a cohesive visual identity, LunarTech aims to make shopping for a complete setup feel approachable and personal.

Colour Palette

LunarTech uses a cool blue palette to support a modern gaming and technology atmosphere. The darker blues can provide strong backgrounds and contrast, while the lighter blue can be used as an accent for interactive elements, highlights, and important information.

Imagery and Art Direction

We agreed on a lunar and space theme when deciding on the company's name and plan to base the website around it. We looked into various websites that also sell computer accessories for inspiration. Among them were...

  • Logitech
  • Logitech Gaming
  • Anker
  • Corsair
    • We took inspiration from them for fonts, and plan to do so for layouts and such.

    We were particularly interested in having categories on the home page displayed like these with each box being a category.

    As for the products and listings, we were inspired by how these looked and want to use the layouts of the content as guidance on which components to include and where to place them.

    For the products themselves, we decided to keep consistency by using photos of the products in an environment (as opposed to being in a blank background). As stock images of computer accessories were not really what we were looking for, we have decided to source photos elsewhere with proper credits given.
